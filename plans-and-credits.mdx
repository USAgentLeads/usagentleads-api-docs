title: "Plans and credits"
description: "One shared credit balance across dashboard, REST, and MCP."
---

| Plan | Monthly | Annual total | Credits per month | API |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | — | 50 | None |
| Starter | $49 | $468 | 1,000 | None |
| Growth | $149 | $1,428 | 5,000 | Read access |
| Pro | $399 | $3,828 | 20,000 | Read, unlock, MCP |

Annual billing grants 12 months of credits upfront. Paid credits expire at the billing period end. Free credits reset each UTC calendar month and do not roll over. There are no credit top-up packs.

One credit unlocks an agent; two unlock a brokerage. Already-owned records and records without email or phone cost zero. Unlocking gives all available record fields; missing fields are not invented. Re-exporting owned contacts spends no additional credits.

API request quotas are separate from credits: Growth and legacy Pro API have 10,000 requests per UTC month; Pro has 100,000. API v2 and MCP share an account limit of 60 requests per minute. MCP protocol requests also count toward request usage.

One-time state, county, brokerage, national, and custom-list purchases remain separate purchases with their existing download terms. They do not add subscription credits or automatically unlock equivalent dashboard rows. Legacy Pro Dashboard and Pro API subscriptions retain their prices and map to Starter and Growth allowances respectively. Manage changes and cancellation through the Stripe billing portal.
