---
title: "Search and recovery"
description: "Filters, record identity, snapshots, and safe request recovery."
---

V3 exposes separate agent and brokerage endpoints. Geography and availability filters retain dashboard names: `state`, `countyFips`, `city` (requires state), `zip`, `mustHave`, and `organization`. Agents also support `activeLicense`; brokerages support `orgType`. Unsupported audience filters are rejected. `mustHave` accepts email, phone, address, license and fullAddress. Name/domain fuzzy matching and sales-volume filters are not available.

Ownership uses the catalog's stable ID within each audience. A source identity correction can produce a new ID; it is not a guaranteed universal person/company identifier. An agent's organization text is not a guaranteed foreign key to a brokerage record.

Successful replay selection and the pinned snapshot stay fixed. Later account suppression changes can withhold records from replay/download; original charges are not rewritten. `withheld_since_delivery` reports this. Suppression changes and new ownership can alter later pages when `exclude_owned` is enabled, but ordinal cursors do not revisit earlier rows.

Retain snapshots while cursors (24 hours) or queued exports reference them. Removed snapshots and expired cursors return 410. Saved delivery payloads are independent of catalog retention and expire after 30 days.
