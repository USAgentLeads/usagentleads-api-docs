---
title: "Agent skills"
description: "Install a focused data workflow for your coding agent."
---

Install `skills/usagentleads` from [USAgentLeads/usagentleads-agent-skills](https://github.com/USAgentLeads/usagentleads-agent-skills) with your agent's skill installer. Set `USAGENTLEADS_API_KEY` in its environment, then invoke `$usagentleads`.

The skill includes a standard-library Python client and guidance on selecting an audience, confirming credit spend, preserving idempotency keys, and exporting with suppression and attribution. It does not authorize outreach messages or claim that contacts are verified for deliverability.
